Ìri's Journal

Editorial Brand Development & Product Founder

Brand Strategy · Editorial Direction · Community Management

2023 - 2024

Ìri's Journal is a creative publication built to give space to voices that don't fit traditional editorial molds. Founded in Chicago in November 2023, the journal publishes personal essays, cultural commentary, photography, and creative writing, prioritizing authenticity and perspective.

Every issue is a collaboration between 15–20 writers, photographers, and artists of various mediums. The editorial process is built around preserving contributor voice: open submissions, transparent selection, and a rule that nothing publishes without the creator's final approval. Distribution runs through contributor networks rather than paid reach, which means the community itself is the growth engine.
