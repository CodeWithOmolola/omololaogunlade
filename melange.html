Watermelon meets rose water in the spirit of Persian cuisine. Cantaloupe is lifted by cracked black pepper, a nod to Italian antipasto. Every flavor is rooted in real food culture. Our organic oat and coconut cream base keeps things clean — no gums, no fillers, no fake flavors — just thoughtful combinations that taste like they were always meant to exist.

Mélange is made for food-forward people who treat their palate as seriously as their values. We sit between boutique artisan and mass-market non-dairy utilizing premium ingredients and culinary intention at an accessible price. Each pint is a study in balance, texture, and unexpected harmony. We want every bite to feel like a discovery.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Launch

Summer Launch. Farmers markets first.

Peak melon season launch via pop-ups and specialty grocers in Portland, Austin, Brooklyn, and SF. Build the brand story through sampling before scaling.

Year 1 — Indie grocers & co-ops Year 2 — Whole Foods regional Year 3 — National + Target

Market Approach

Flavor education over advertising.

Content explains the why behind each pairing. Partner with food bloggers and recipe developers. Position Mélange as an ingredient, not just a dessert.

Flavor-first content Recipe collaborations DTC super-fan drops

Pricing

$6.99–7.99 per pint.

Intentionally between mass-market ($4.99) and boutique ($9.99) — accessible luxury that reflects organic ingredients and small-batch production.
