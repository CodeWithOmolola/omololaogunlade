Allude

Voice Assistant Device Bridging AI and Social Media

Consumer Technology

Product Design · Product Development · Strategy · Market Positioning

AutoCAD · Figma · Market Research

Allude is a product exploring what happens when Google and X build hardware together. The premise: 4.9 billion people use social media, but every current AI assistant ignores it as a knowledge layer. Allude bridges that gap, a keychain-sized device with a dual-screen design and a shake interface borrowed from the Magic 8 Ball. Shake twice for personalized social insights via X's data. Shake three times for factual responses via Google's knowledge base. No tutorial needed. The interaction model is one three generations already know.

The concept spans hardware design, platform-native API integration, and a go-to-market strategy built around nostalgia as interface design. The core question: what does a social-native AI device look like when the phone stays in your pocket and a dedicated layer surfaces the pulse?
