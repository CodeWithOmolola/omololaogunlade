Allude

Voice Assistant Device Bridging AI and Social Media

Social media usage has moved off the screen and into daily life, but voice assistants haven't caught up; they answer factual questions, not "what are people saying/doing right now." Allude is a keychain-sized device built to close that gap: a dual-screen assistant that treats social platforms as a real input, not just a notification source. Shake it, and it answers either from X's real-time social data or Google's knowledge base, depending on what you're asking.

Product Decisions

Interaction

Shake, Don't Type

Voice assistants and typed queries both add friction between "I wonder" and an answer. Allude borrows the Magic 8 Ball's interaction model instead, a gesture three generations already know without a tutorial. Shake twice for social context (X), shake three times for factual information (Google). The form isn't nostalgic for its own sake; it's a proven interaction pattern applied to a new problem.

Interaction Design UX

Architecture

Dual-Screen, Platform-Native

A single display can't hold both a live social feed and an AI response without one degrading the other. Allude splits the two: a primary display for feed and responses, a secondary touchscreen for text input when voice isn't practical. Built on dual OLED displays, a voice-optimized mic array, haptic shake feedback, and native integration with X, TikTok, and Instagram.

Partnership

Why Google & X

Google supplies the knowledge layer, factual, verifiable answers. X supplies the social layer, real-time sentiment and conversation. Neither covers what the other does well. Together, Allude can answer "what's true" and "what are people saying" from the same gesture, which is the actual gap the product is built to close.
