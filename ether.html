ether is a community platform. It started from a simple observation: most platforms sort content by category or by who posted it, optimizing for engagement over substance. ether sorts by intent instead centering what someone's actually trying to do by sharing, and is built around slowing down rather than maximizing time on the app.
The current version(s) is testing user interaction and core capabilities, with the full app targeted for Spring 2027.
