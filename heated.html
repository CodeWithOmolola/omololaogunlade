AutoCAD · Figma · Market Research · Adobe Illustrator & InDesign

Most home objects address one sensory lever at a time, warmth, scent, or form; even though research shows all three shape cognitive state: ambient warmth shifts people toward intuitive thinking, pleasant fragrance improves mood and creative performance, and nature-adjacent forms reduce mental fatigue. Heated addresses all three at once. It's a candle warmer, lamp, and incense holder built around a simple premise: your environment either works for you or against you, and most home goods aren't designed with that in mind.

Product Decisions

Form

Why a Mushroom Shape

Inspired by mycelial networks, the design treats warming, fragrance, and mood as one connected system rather than separate features: the warming system distributes fragrance, the fragrance shifts mood, the mood shifts cognitive state. Research on nature-adjacent forms shows this kind of biophilic cue can lower psychological guard and restore attention depleted by screens and overstimulation; which is the actual function the shape is doing, not just its look.

Design Rationale Biophilic Design

Architecture

Engineered for Safety and Airflow

Dual-function as both candle warmer and incense holder, with dedicated placement zones for each. A ventilation system creates a natural chimney effect for even scent distribution. Precision-engineered ring positioning maximizes heat transfer while keeping the candle out of direct glass contact. Touch-sensitive temperature and LED controls sit on top, so the technical layer stays invisible during use.

Partnership

Why IKEA & Adobe

IKEA's design language is built around everyday life as it's lived. Adobe's is built around the conditions that make creative work possible. Heated sits at the intersection: an accessible object doing the job of a luxury one, aimed at making a home's environment work with someone's creative process instead of against it.
