Heated

Biophilic Candle Warmer, Lamp & Incense Holder

Consumer Home Goods

Product Design · Product Development · Strategy · Market Positioning

AutoCAD · Figma · Market Research

Heated is what happens when IKEA and Adobe share a premise: your environment doesn't just affect how you feel — it shapes how you think. Most home goods address one sensory lever at a time. Heated combines a candle warmer, ambient lamp, and incense holder in a single biophilic form. A mushroom-inspired, warm-toned, designed to engineer the conditions that make focused and creative work feel natural.

The product spans hardware design, a companion app with mood presets, and a go-to-market strategy rooted in environmental psychology research. The core question: what does "accessible luxury" actually look like when democratic design meets creative infrastructure?
