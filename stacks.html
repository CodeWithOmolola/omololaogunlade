Brand Identity · Logo Design · Apparel Design

2024

Stacks is a lifestyle brand built around skateboarding culture and the self-expression that lives inside it. The work spans brand identity and apparel design — logo, and merchandise developed with the visual language of skate: raw, considered, and unbothered by trends it didn't start.

The creative direction pulls from the culture itself rather than looking at it from the outside. Stacks isn't a brand about skating. It's a brand that skates.
