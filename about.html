My path has run through data annotation at Amazon, digital merchandising at Ulta Beauty, editorial direction at Ìri's Journal, and now building ether and oogun.works, my consultancy & practice that ties the rest together.

I studied Behavioral Science, with a minor in Biology & Research. That's still the lens I bring to every project: people first, systems second, and operations as the thing that makes either of those real.
