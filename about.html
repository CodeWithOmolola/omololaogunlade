I'm a Chicago native based in Brooklyn, NY. I study how people make decisions, how systems shape those decisions, and what becomes possible when you understand both.

My work moves between research, strategy, and execution. That might mean developing a product, shaping a brand, designing an operational system, or exploring how emerging technology can better serve people. My experience spans data operations at Amazon, digital merchandising at Ulta Beauty, editorial direction at Ìri's Journal, and speculative product design through Modern Blnd.

Today, that work comes together through my strategy and consulting practice, and ether, my exploration of community, technology, and digital products. Different expressions of the same instinct: understand the problem beneath the surface, find the opportunity, and build what comes next.

I studied Behavioral Science, with a minor in Biology. It's still the lens I bring to every project: people first, systems second, and operations as the discipline that makes either real.
